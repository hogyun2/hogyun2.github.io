Hi, I'm Hogyun Kim.

Scalable and collaborative autonomy for multi-robot systems

I'm a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Inha University, advised by Younggun Cho in SPARO Lab. I also received my B.S. in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering from Inha University. My research focuses on scalable and collaborative robot autonomy in multi-robot systems, studying how robots perceive, map, and operate in shared environments through communication-efficient coordination and interaction. For my personal thoughts and research experiences, please check out my Posts :)

    Research

    Selected research

    My main line of work is multi-robot SLAM: helping a team of robots build one consistent map under limited bandwidth and computing. These papers cover lightweight place recognition for finding shared places, communication-efficient map merging, and distributed SLAM deployed in the field.

    Research Thread

    Imaging Radar

    Read the post

    Radar sees through rain, snow, fog, and dust, but its scans are full of speckle and multipath. I build radar place recognition and global localization that stay lightweight enough to share between robots, as a step toward radar-based multi-robot SLAM.

    2025.09 – PresentPrincipal InvestigatorNRF Grant

    Building a Real-Time Multi-Robot SLAM Framework in Communication-Constrained Scenarios

    통신 제약 상황에서 실시간 다중 로봇 SLAM 프레임워크 구축

    National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) · Ph.D. Candidate Research Fellowship

    Most multi-robot systems assume reliable communication and break down under the delays, dropouts, and limited bandwidth of real sites. This project builds a multi-robot SLAM framework that keeps robots collaborating while exchanging only the minimum necessary information.

    • Year 1Lossless information-exchange policy on an exchange graph, built on lightweight global descriptors and sequence matching
    • Year 2Submap-based inter-robot registration and distributed back-end optimization for globally consistent mapping
    • Year 3Datasets and hardware with real communication logs, full-system integration, and field validation
